Using the app

Use Ferah to organise photos and videos you allow it to access. You can change access in device settings. These terms do not limit your statutory consumer rights.

When you delete an item

Marking an item for deletion does not delete it from your gallery. Deletion needs your confirmation. Undo changes your in-app choice; it cannot recover files deleted by the system. Back up what you want to keep first. With sync enabled, deletion may affect other devices too.

Displayed file sizes do not measure the space freed on your phone or cloud account.

When you share an item

Share only content you have the right to share. Photos and videos may include dates, location or other metadata. Ferahladım images are created on your device; you choose whether to save or share them.

Ferah Plus and subscriptions

The purchase screen shows the Plus price, duration and renewal terms. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Apple handles payments. Cancel in Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Restore checks valid purchases on the same Apple Account. Refund requests follow Apple’s process and your legal rights.
