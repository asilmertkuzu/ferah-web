Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-01

Publisher: ASİL MERT KUZU · Contact: ferahcleaner@gmail.com

FERAH does not send gallery content, search or scan results, or cleanup statistics to its servers. It does not sell your photos or use them for advertising.

Scope

This policy covers FERAH’s first iOS release, its privacy and support pages, and support correspondence. Android and Google Photos guidance in the Storage Guide is general information, not a statement that an Android app has been released. Data use for an Android release will be explained before it launches.

Access to your photos

You choose which photos and videos FERAH can access. With limited access, we work only with the items you select. Full access lets you review the library covered by that permission. Turning access off makes features that need your gallery unavailable.

To show and sort items, FERAH uses their content, filename, identifier, creation and modification dates, type, dimensions, video duration and any file size it can read. This information is processed on your phone.

To change photo access, open iOS Settings from Manage selection or Photo access in FERAH. The app has separate controls for cleanup sounds and vibration. Video audio is managed in the player. FERAH does not record from your microphone.

Storage information

Your device capacity and available space are read from iOS and displayed on your device. This information is not sent to a FERAH server. FERAH does not access your iCloud account’s storage usage.

File size totals are calculated from photos and videos whose size can be read. They do not measure space freed on your device or in iCloud. Local copies, Recently Deleted and iCloud Photos settings can affect available space.

Your settings and choices

Pending deletion choices and the last deletion’s file identifiers, revision information and outcome are saved on your phone. If the app closes, this record lets it check the library again; inaccessible files are not treated as deleted. This record contains no photo or video content and is not sent to a server.

Your language, theme, sound and vibration settings are saved on your phone. FERAH also remembers whether you have seen the first-use guide and your Keep, Favorite and Separate choices. Choices are linked to the photo or video’s identifier. Priority for items returned to Recently Saved is saved too.

Recent Activity keeps up to 50 decisions and their times. Cleanup History stores on-device summaries of successful individual, bulk and duplicate cleanups: counts, file sizes and times. These summaries contain no photo or video content. Undoing a decision updates its decision record. Revoking photo access does not automatically erase local records.

Photos and videos in iCloud

An item’s original file may be stored only in iCloud. Viewing, playing, copying, sharing it or reading its exact size may require Apple to download the file to your phone. This can use an internet connection and temporary disk space, and may take some time. Your existing Photos and iCloud sync settings still apply.

Smart Duplicate Cleanup

Exact duplicates are found by comparing files and similar photos by comparing the visual features of small previews. Both happen on your device. Dates, locations and favorite information already stored with photos may also be checked. Your device’s current location is not requested. Scan results are held temporarily in memory.

The duplicate scan does not download files from iCloud itself. It may skip items that cannot be read on your phone or compared safely. iCloud downloads started by another operation may continue.

Time Machine

Capture dates are used to find permitted photos and videos from the same day in earlier years. The search runs on your device.

Cleanup

The story shows completed deletion counts and total file sizes from local records. If some sizes are unknown, the total is shown as at least that amount. The size is omitted when no sizes were recorded.

To avoid counting the same media twice, the statistics record stores a token derived from its identifier and the first review time locally. This token is not the photo itself and the statistics record does not store the raw media identifier.

Statistics are kept over time so you can see your progress. Past activity that was never recorded is not recreated. Plus expiry does not erase cleanup history or statistics.

Storage guide

Explains how to manage device and cloud storage on iPhone and Android. It does not sign in to iCloud or Google accounts or read their usage. Opening an official help link connects your browser to that service’s website.

Temporary files and saved information

File information, sizes, display addresses and small previews may be kept in memory or the phone’s cache so items open faster. We try to clean up temporary files created to read sizes when the operation finishes. A file may remain if work is interrupted or iOS keeps it in a cache.

On iOS, you can remove FERAH and its data in Settings → General → iPhone Storage → FERAH → Delete App. Offload App keeps documents and data. Deleting FERAH does not delete photos or videos in your library. Data remaining in system backups and its restoration depend on your device backup settings.

When you delete an item

Swiping left adds a photo or video to FERAH’s Deleted Items list. Deleting it from your library requires confirmation in FERAH and then in iOS. Undo changes a swipe decision. It cannot recover an item deleted through iOS.

Apple Photos generally keeps deleted items in Recently Deleted for 30 days. You can check the remaining time and recover an item there when possible. FERAH cannot restore a permanently deleted item; we do not keep a server backup. If iCloud Photos is on, deletion may sync to other devices using the same Apple Account.

When you share an item

When you choose to share, you select the app or person in the iOS share sheet. Photos and videos may be passed to the chosen service with embedded information such as dates or locations. That service’s privacy terms apply.

You can share the story image currently displayed in Cleanup. It is created on your device and contains no personal photos or videos from your library. Choosing Save adds a new copy to Photos with the required permission.

Only the selected item is prepared as a temporary copy for sharing. FERAH attempts to remove this copy when the share sheet closes; it may remain in the cache if the app closes unexpectedly. Sharing does not remove the item from your gallery or FERAH lists.

Accounts, ads and Plus

You do not need a FERAH account in this version. It has no advertising network, advertising tracking or third-party analytics that monitor your use. Ferah Plus subscriptions, payments and restoration are managed through the Apple App Store. FERAH reads Apple’s verified transaction and subscription status on the device to confirm Plus access. FERAH does not receive or store card details. If we add a service that changes how information is used, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy details before making it available.

Email support and this website

If you email us, we use your email address, message and anything you attach to understand the issue and reply. You do not need to send private photos or videos. You can use the same address for requests about keeping or deleting correspondence. Our support address uses Gmail, and your email is sent through your own email provider.

These pages have no contact form, ads or analytics tracking. Your theme preference may be saved in your browser. The hosting service may keep technical records such as IP addresses, connection details and errors. Your gallery content is not uploaded to this website. Hosting and email services also have their own privacy terms.

Questions

For privacy questions or concerns, email ferahcleaner@gmail.com. We will update the date on this page when we make an important change to this policy.
